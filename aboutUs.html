         The Animals’ Welfare and Protection Association (AWPA) was formed and registered under the
         Companies’ Ordinance in 1963 by a small group of animal lovers, concerned by the distressing 
         conditions that stray animals are subject to in Sri Lanka. Thereafter, the Association was certified as
         a voluntary organization under the Voluntary Social Service Organization Act No. 31 of 1980.
